Our History & Mission

Founded in late 2024 as a small university project, ShopNest grew out of a simple vision: to make online shopping clean, straightforward, and accessible to everyone without cluttered corporate layouts.

🌱 How We Started

What began as a localized script written in a university dorm room has now evolved into a fully functional mock e-commerce platform. Our goal was to learn full-stack web development fundamentals from scratch—focusing on clean HTML, semantic CSS structure, and responsive JavaScript design.

🎯 Our Core Values

We believe that simplicity beats complexity. Every product displayed on our store is hand-picked to ensure quality, reliability, and value. We prioritize transparent pricing, fast local data storage management, and a seamless user interface.
